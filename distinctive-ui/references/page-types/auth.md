# Authentication

Design the form as the primary task.

Possible visual directions:
- editorial split screen;
- 3D product object;
- atmospheric dark scene;
- premium glass panel;
- illustrated world;
- quiet monochrome.

Do not sacrifice trust or form usability for spectacle.

Include:
- validation;
- password visibility;
- loading state;
- errors;
- accessible labels;
- recovery path;
- social auth if required.
