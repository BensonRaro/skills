# Luxury / Tactile

Luxury design relies on:
- typography;
- spacing;
- material imagery;
- restrained color;
- precise alignment;
- high-quality product presentation.

Use dark charcoal, warm neutrals, metallic accents, cream, or restrained monochrome where appropriate.

Distinctive adaptation:
For ecommerce, treat the product page like a gallery: one hero object, controlled information, large details, and strong material photography.
