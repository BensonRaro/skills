# Image Sourcing and Licensing

When the local inspiration pool does not contain suitable production imagery:

1. Search for the subject and desired art direction.
2. Prefer a clearly licensed source.
3. For photography, Unsplash can be a useful source for many commercial and personal website uses.
4. Verify the source and the specific image before treating it as production-ready.
5. Avoid recognizable logos, artworks, branded products and people when rights are unclear.
6. Do not bulk scrape or compile image libraries.
7. Keep source attribution/metadata in the project when practical.

Important:
A free image license does not automatically grant rights to every depicted trademark, artwork, property or person's likeness.

Inspiration screenshots are references, not automatically reusable production assets.
